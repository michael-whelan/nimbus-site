Nimbus

Weather that answers the question you actually asked.

Do I need sunscreen? Will it rain? How hot will it get? Nimbus keeps the UV index next to temperature and rainfall instead of burying it three screens deep — on your home screen, without opening the app.

What it does

  • UV first

    Current UV, today's peak, and the hourly forecast — shown with the same colour scale throughout, so a glance is enough.

  • Home screen widgets

    Temperature, conditions and UV on your home screen. Most days you should not need to open the app at all.

  • Saved locations

    Fast search and favourites, so checking the weather where your family lives takes one tap.

  • Works offline

    The last forecast is cached on your device and shown immediately while a fresh one loads.

Free, and staying that way

No adverts. No tracking. No accounts. No subscription, and no features held back behind one. Nimbus asks for your location only to fetch the forecast for where you are, and your saved places never leave your phone.

If it earns a place on your home screen and you want to support the work, there is a coffee link — entirely optional.
