Release Notes

What changed in each version of Nimbus, newest first.

1.0.4

Nimbus is smaller to download and install.

Also: searching with no connection now explains what went wrong instead of showing a technical error, and the Widgets button tells you how to add the widget to your home screen.

1.0.3

Today's weather symbol now shows what is happening right now, rather than one summary standing in for the whole day — on the main screen and on the home screen widget. Tomorrow and later days still show a summary for the day.

1.0.2

The home screen widget now adapts to the size you make it — the text and icon scale with the widget, and the UV reading no longer gets cut off when it is small.

Also: a place saved from search now shows as saved everywhere in the app, and if location access is off, Nimbus tells you instead of quietly showing nothing.

1.0.1

The home screen widget now refreshes on its own in the background, instead of waiting until you next open the app.

Also: switching between °C and °F is instant, the keyboard gets out of the way when you pick a place, and the app is noticeably smaller.

1.0.0

Initial release.
